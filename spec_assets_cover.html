DUOLITH-KEM:
A Dual-Wall Post-Quantum
Key Encapsulation Mechanism
Two independent lattice walls — a structured Module-LWE layer over the cyclotomic ring Z7681[X]/(X256+1) and an unstructured plain-LWE layer modulo 65536 — whose independently encapsulated secrets are combined with SHA-3 and lifted to IND-CCA2 security by the implicit-rejection Fujisaki-Okamoto transform. The shared secret stays protected whenever at least one wall holds.
DUOLITH-KEM Design Team
Security estimates computed with the Albrecht–Player–Samuels lattice estimator; correctness bounds proven by exact MGF Chernoff analysis; reference implementation with 28-test suite and deterministic known-answer vectors.
